This file is the other half of the issue: the real hit songs a Taipei visitor would recognise, one list per decade, and what it would take to play any of them on the public page. **Nothing on this list is in the repository, and nothing goes in until CJ picks and the licences are in hand.** CJ, 2026-09-22: 「i want music for different section can you find 1980s 1990s 2000s 2010s music and make a music player system, i can switch when i am in that decade」.

## What licensing a real song takes

A published web page that plays a recording needs two separate permissions, from two different owners, and neither is covered by the collecting societies' blanket licences:

1. **Synchronisation licence for the composition** (music and lyrics), from the music publisher. In Taiwan the performance right is administered by MÜST (社團法人中華音樂著作權協會), but a sync for a web page is negotiated directly with the publisher, not through MÜST.
2. **Master use licence for the recording**, from the label that owns the master (or the artist, where they own it).

Realistic expectations for a non-commercial Build Day page: a small label or self-owned artist may say yes for a nominal fee or for free with credit, in a couple of weeks; a major (Sony, Warner, Universal, JVR, B'in) usually quotes a fee and takes a month or more. A cover or re-recording removes the master licence but still needs the sync licence. Using only the song title as text on the page needs nothing. There is no "30-second" or "non-commercial" exemption in Taiwanese or US copyright law that would cover a public GitHub Pages site.

The cheapest route that still names real songs: show the real title and artist in the control as the decade's "memory song" (text only), while the synthesised loop plays. That needs no licence and is a one-line change to `music.js`.

Labels and publishers below are the ones on the original release; ownership moves, so confirm the current holder before writing to anyone. Years are release years.

## Recommendation, one line each

Take the text-only route now: the control shows the real title and artist under the synthesised loop, no licence needed, shippable today. If CJ wants real audio, ask 茄子蛋 (浪子回頭) and 草東 (大風吹) first, both single small owners, and 羅大佑 (戀曲1980) for the 80s; a yes from any one of them is a story for the Build Day pitch. Do not start with Jay Chou, Jolin or the majors: a month and a fee for a page with no revenue.
